```
Color Match: Memory Game is a simple, offline color-memory game. A color is shown for a few seconds, then the player must pick the exact same color among several very similar cards. The player has 3 lives, and levels get harder as cards become more alike and more numerous. It trains short-term visual memory and color perception and is aimed at casual players of all ages who want a quick, focused game with no sign-up, ads or purchases.

How to review: No login, demo account or sample files are required. Launch the app, tap "Play", memorize the color shown (tap "Ready" to continue early), then tap the matching card. The game ends after 3 mistakes and shows the score and level reached. The best score is stored locally on the device. Language (EN/TR) and music can be changed from the home screen.

External services: None. No backend, authentication, analytics, advertising, payment or AI services. The app works fully offline and collects no personal data. It only stores the language, music setting and best score locally on the device.

Regional differences: None. The app functions identically in all regions. Available in English and Turkish.

Regulated industry / third-party material: Not applicable. All music and sound effects are original (generated by the developer).

Account features: The app has no account registration, login, account deletion, user-generated content or paid content.

A screen recording captured on a physical iPhone (latest iOS) was provided in the Resolution Center.
```
